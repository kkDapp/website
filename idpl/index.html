A Transparent, Multi-City League

Competition Format

01

Registrations

The journey begins with online registrations through the official IDPL platform, verified before confirmation.

02

City Auditions

Auditions across selected cities, judged on technique, creativity, synchronization, stage presence, musicality and overall performance.

03

City Qualifiers

Professionally produced city-level competitions with celebrity appearances, guest performances and media coverage.

04

City Finals

Best performers compete for the City Championship — trophy, prize money, certificates and national qualification.

05

National Grand Finale

City champions compete on one stage for the IDPL Championship — celebrity judges, live audience, national media, digital streaming.
